# Improving the guide

Corrections to reading notes, source references, transcriptions, and navigation are welcome. This is a documentation repository. Research implementation changes belong in the authors' repository under its contribution process.

For a factual correction, give the exact paper version, section, PDF page, figure/table, and the proposed correction. Explain whether the issue is a transcription error in this companion or an inconsistency in the paper. Retain important source discrepancies rather than silently choosing a convenient value.

Keep reported findings, interpretations, and open questions distinct. Every new numerical claim needs its metric, scope, comparator, aggregation unit, uncertainty where reported, and material caveats. Do not claim an experiment was reproduced unless actual execution evidence is added and reviewed as a separate extension.

Keep all seven authors credited. Do not add the companion maintainer to the paper's authorship. Use original explanatory prose and preserve third-party licence scope. Run `python scripts/check_repository.py`, then review the affected facts against the source.
